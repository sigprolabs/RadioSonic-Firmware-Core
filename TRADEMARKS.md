  and then sold, given away or otherwise passed to someone else, must not
  display "RadioSonic", "SigPro Labs", "SigPro Labs, LLC" or "sigprolabs.com"
  anywhere on it: not on the silkscreen, copper, enclosure, packaging or
  labels. This applies to plain text as well as to any logo or stylized form,
  and it includes copyright lines and web addresses. Where the published board
  artwork contains any of these, such as the company name and web address in
  the silkscreen, they identify boards made by SigPro Labs, LLC. They are not
  copyright or license notices. Remove them before manufacture. This includes
  group orders and boards made for classmates, colleagues or customers.
- **Name your product or project after ours.** Do not use RadioSonic or
  SigPro Labs, or anything confusingly similar, in the name of a product,
  company, project, domain or social media account.
- **Identify your device as ours.** Firmware that you distribute must not
  report the RadioSonic or SigPro Labs names in its product or manufacturer
  strings, and must not use USB vendor or product IDs assigned to
  SigPro Labs, LLC.
- **Suggest endorsement.** Do not state or imply that SigPro Labs, LLC makes,
  sponsors, supports or endorses your product, course or service.

## What you may do without asking

- **Build a board for yourself.** A board that you make for your own personal
  or educational use, and keep, may carry the names exactly as they appear in
  the published design files. If you later sell it or give it away, remove
  the names first.
- **Keep our notices in the files.** The copyright and license notices in the
  design and firmware files, including the line
  "Copyright (c) 2026 SigPro Labs, LLC", must stay in those files, as the
  licenses require. Keeping a required notice in a file is not a use of our
  trademarks. Do not reproduce those notices on the board itself.
- **Pass the notices on with your boards.** The CERN-OHL-P v2 requires that
  whoever receives a board from you has access to the notices. Meet that
  through the documentation you supply with the board or a link to the design
  files, not through text on the board.
- **Refer to our products truthfully.** For example: "compatible with the
  RadioSonic board", "based on the RadioSonic design by SigPro Labs, LLC", or
  "a fork of the RadioSonic firmware".
